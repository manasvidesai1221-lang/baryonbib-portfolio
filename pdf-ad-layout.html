Exploring the evolution of a dwarf spheroidal galaxy with SPH simulations: II. AGN feedback
Roberto Hazenfrats1, Paramita Barai2, Gustavo A. Lanfranchi1, and Anderson Caproni1
1Department of Astronomy, University of São Paulo, São Paulo, SP, Brazil
2Institute of Theoretical Physics, University of Warsaw, Warsaw, Poland

ABSTRACT
We investigate AGN feedback from an intermediate-mass black hole at the center of a dwarf spheroidal galaxy, by performing isolated galaxy simulations using a modified version of the GADGET-3 code. Our simulations incorporate both mechanical and radiative feedback modes, allowing us to study their relative importance in regulating star formation and affecting the galaxy's morphological evolution. We find that AGN feedback plays a crucial role in determining the final stellar mass and dark matter distribution of dwarf spheroidal galaxies, particularly in systems with initial masses above 10⁸ M⊙.
